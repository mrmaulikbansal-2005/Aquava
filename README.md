# Aquava

**Strava for water.** A mobile web app that turns saving water into a team sport for Arizona. Take a quick habits quiz, see your daily water profile, fix leaks, follow AI tips built on real AZMet weather data, and climb live leaderboards with your friends. Ranked on progress, not gallons.

Every drop. Every neighbor. One goal.

CIS 415 Big Data & AI in Business prototype by Devansh Purohit & Maulik Bansal, Arizona State University.
